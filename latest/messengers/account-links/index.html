Link your messenger accounts¶

An Account link ties your Telegram account, your account on a Mattermost server, or your account on a messenger that Muster reaches through a Bridge, to your Muster User. With it, a button you press under an Alert Group in a messenger runs as you — the Timeline names you and shows the messenger you pressed in — and Mentions and footers in the messenger name you.

A Telegram account belongs to one User across every Telegram Connection. A Mattermost account belongs to one User per Mattermost Connection, since each Connection is its own server, and so does an account reached through a Bridge, per Bridge Connection. You have at most one Telegram account and one account per Mattermost or Bridge Connection; linking another one replaces the earlier link, and linking the same account again keeps its link and updates its username and name.

Why links start in your profile¶

Every link starts in your signed-in Muster session, and the secret travels from there to your messenger account — never the other way. Nobody can link your account by typing your name into a chat, and nobody can link their account to your User: only the account that receives the secret Muster gave you in your own session gets linked. That is why linking needs no extra password or TOTP code, and why an API token, an Admin's included, cannot start, confirm or remove a link of its own User.

Link Telegram¶

  1. In your profile, open Messenger accounts, choose the Telegram Connection and select Link Telegram — or Relink Telegram once you have a Telegram link, which the new link replaces.
  2. Muster shows a link https://t.me/<bot>?start=<token>. Open it on the device where you use Telegram, within 10 minutes; the link works once.
  3. Telegram opens a chat with the bot. Press Start.
  4. The bot answers "Linked to Muster user {your name} ({your login})", and your profile shows the link at once.

If the bot answers "This link is invalid or expired. Start again from your Muster profile.", the link was used already, expired, belongs to another Telegram bot of Muster, or was never issued: start again from your profile. If it answers "This account is linked to another user. Its owner or an admin can unlink it.", that Telegram account is linked to someone else; nothing changed.

Link Mattermost¶

  1. In your profile, open Messenger accounts, choose the Mattermost Connection, enter your Mattermost username — with or without the @ — and select Send code.
  2. The Connection's bot sends you a direct message: "You requested a link to Muster. Do not share this code." with an 8-character code. If you did not ask for it, ignore it.
  3. Type the code into your profile within 10 minutes; capital and small letters are the same. The bot confirms "Linked to Muster user {your name}".

A code allows 5 attempts; after five wrong codes it stops working, even with the right code, and you request a new one. A new request replaces your earlier one. You can request 5 codes an hour, and one Mattermost account gets at most 3 codes an hour, whoever asks; beyond that, wait until the oldest request is an hour old. A username the server does not know, a bot account and a deactivated account cannot be linked. An account that is linked to another User is refused.

The bot must be allowed to open direct messages with you; see Direct messages.

Link through a Bridge¶

A Bridge Connection reaches a messenger through a bridge, which tells Muster in its info what it can do for Account links. Every bridge takes a token that you send to its bot. A bridge that can look people up and start a direct chat with them also offers a code that its bot sends you, as Mattermost does. Your profile offers what the Connection's bridge declares.

Once linked, a Mention of you in a message of that Connection names you by the name the bridge reported for your account, which a bridge with native mentions turns into its messenger's mention, and the footer of a message you acknowledged, snoozed or resolved names you by that name. Without a link, a Mention is your Muster name as plain text, which notifies nobody.

By a token sent to the bot¶

  1. In your profile, open Messenger accounts, choose the Bridge Connection and select Link.
  2. Muster shows a token of 43 characters and, when the bridge declares a link that opens a chat with its bot, that link as well. Within 10 minutes, send the token to the bot of that Connection in a direct message, or open the link, which hands the token to the bot. The token works once, and only with the bot of the Connection you chose.
  3. The bot answers "Linked to Muster user {your name} ({your login})", and your profile shows the link with the name and the handle the bridge reported for your account.

If the bot answers "This link is invalid or expired. Start again from your Muster profile.", the token was used already, expired, was started for another Connection — another bridge, or another bot of the same bridge — or was never issued: start again from your profile. If it answers "This account is linked to another user. Its owner or an admin can unlink it.", that account is linked to someone else; nothing changed.

By a code by direct message¶

The code is offered only when the bridge declares how it finds people — its lookup kind, one of email address, login, username or user id — and that its bot can start a direct chat. Your profile names the field after that kind: Email, Login, Username or User id.

  1. In your profile, choose the Bridge Connection, enter your email address, login, username or user id in the messenger, as the field asks, and select Send code.
  2. The bot sends you a direct message: "You requested a link to Muster. Do not share this code." with an 8-character code. If you did not ask for it, ignore it.
  3. Type the code into your profile within 10 minutes; capital and small letters are the same. The link is made at once, and the bot sends no confirmation.

If the bridge finds no one, Muster answers "No account with this email was found." — or login, username or user id. If the bot may not write to you, Muster answers "The bot cannot send you a direct message. Send it the token instead." The limits are those of Mattermost: 5 attempts per code, a new request replaces your earlier one, 5 code requests an hour for you and at most 3 codes an hour for one account, whoever asks. An account that is linked to another User is refused.

Several Connections of one messenger¶

An Organization may have several Connections of one messenger, such as two Mattermost bots on one server or a second Telegram bot. What a link covers differs by messenger:

  • Mattermost links are per Connection. Each Mattermost Connection is its own Identity space, even when two Connections use bots on the same server: two bots on one server need two links, made one after the other from Link Mattermost with each Connection. Your profile lists one row per Connection.
  • Bridge links are per Connection. Each Bridge Connection is its own Identity space, also when two Connections reach the same bridge with two bots: two Connections need two links, made one after the other, and a token started for one of them links nothing when you send it to the other's bot. Your profile lists one row per Connection.
  • A Telegram link is valid for all Telegram bots. Telegram has one Identity space, so the link you make through any Telegram Connection lets you press buttons sent by every Telegram bot of Muster. Your profile shows it as "Telegram · @ · for all Telegram bots", with "linked via " below.

One chat reached through two Connections is two Destinations, and each posts its own message for every Alert Group. This is allowed, but the Route editor warns when two or more of a Route's Destinations deliver to the same chat — one Telegram chat, or one Mattermost server and channel — since their messages appear twice. The Destinations list, the Route editor and the Alert Group page name each Destination's Connection and channel, so you can tell them apart.

Unlink¶

In your profile, under Messenger accounts, remove the link. Nothing can be unlinked from a chat. Removing a link, like making one, is recorded in the Audit log with who did it and from where.

What Admins can do¶

Admins see the links of every User on the user page, under Account links, and can remove any of them — their own from the web session only, as above. They cannot create a link for someone else. Deleting a User removes their links and voids the links they had started.

What a press answers¶

Who pressed What happens The private answer
An account without a link Nothing changes "Your Telegram account is not linked to Muster. Link it in your profile: {link}" — or the same for Mattermost, and "Your account is not linked to Muster. Link it in your profile: {link}" through a Bridge; the link goes to your profile and carries no secret
A linked Viewer Nothing changes "You are not permitted to do this"
A linked User who is disabled Nothing changes "Your Muster account is disabled"
A linked Responder or Admin The Command runs as that User "Done: {command}", or why it was refused

The Audit log records account_link.link, account_link.unlink and account_link.link_rejected with the User, the messenger, the account's id and username, and the Connection; a rejection says why: token_unknown, token_used, token_expired, wrong_connection, wrong_code or conflict.
