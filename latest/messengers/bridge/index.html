Connect a messenger through a bridge¶

A bridge connects Muster to a messenger that Muster does not support natively. It is a separate service that speaks the Bridge protocol with Muster on one side and talks to your messenger on the other: Muster sends it every message as an envelope — the status, the title, a link, the text and the buttons — and the bridge renders the envelope in its messenger. A Bridge Connection is one bot of your messenger behind one bridge: one token is one bot. Muster posts one Root message per Alert Group to a channel of that messenger, edits it as the Alert Group changes, and replies in its Thread for what happens later, as it does for Mattermost and Telegram.

In the examples, MUSTER_PUBLIC_URL is https://muster.example.org, MUSTER_INGEST_URL is https://ingest.muster.example.org and the bridge listens on https://bridge.example.org. The web UI has no form for Bridge Connections and Bridge Destinations yet, so the examples create them through the API, with a Personal access token of an Admin in MUSTER_TOKEN.

Create the Connection¶

From your bridge, you need:

  • its base URL, an http or https address with an optional path prefix, such as https://bridge.example.org/muster. Muster sends every request to <base URL>/v1/…, and only there;
  • the token it gave your bot. Muster sends it as Authorization: Bearer on every request, stores it encrypted and never shows it again.

Choose how the bridge reports events — the presses of buttons and the messages people write in a Thread — with the event delivery mode:

  • poll when the bridge cannot reach Muster. The replica that leads asks the bridge for new events, so the bridge needs no address of Muster.
  • push when the bridge can reach MUSTER_INGEST_URL. The bridge posts each event to the Connection's callback address, https://ingest.muster.example.org/api/v1/callbacks/bridge/CN…, which the Connection shows in callback_url, signed with a push secret that you configure on both sides. The push secret has the Standard Webhooks form, whsec_ followed by base64, and is write-only like the token.

A Connection also has a proxy, for a bridge that Muster reaches only through one, and a limiter: 10 requests per second by default, shared by all its Destinations. When the bridge answers that it is rate-limited, Muster waits as long as it asks, whatever the limiter allows.

curl -X POST https://muster.example.org/api/v1/connections \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MUSTER_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"type":"bridge","name":"Team chat","base_url":"https://bridge.example.org/muster",
       "bot_token":"the-token-of-the-bot","event_delivery":"poll","proxy":{"enabled":false},
       "limiter":{"limit":10,"per_seconds":1}}'

For push, set "event_delivery":"push" and add "push_secret":"whsec_…".

What the check shows¶

Check the Connection after you create it, and after every change of the bridge:

curl -X POST https://muster.example.org/api/v1/connections/CNK7M3QX9P2RTA/checks \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MUSTER_TOKEN"

The check calls the bridge's getInfo and shows two steps, each with its latency and whether it went through the proxy:

  1. info: the bridge's name and version, the messenger's name, the protocol version — Muster speaks 1 — what the bridge offers, such as buttons and Thread messages, its limits, such as the longest text of a message, how it finds people for Account links, and its event delivery modes. Muster keeps this copy on the Connection and reads it again before a call that depends on it once it is older than 10 minutes, so a change in the bridge reaches Muster without a restart.
  2. event_delivery: whether the bridge offers the Connection's event delivery mode.

A failed step names its cause with a code:

Code Cause
token_invalid The bridge refused the token.
protocol_version_unknown The bridge speaks a protocol version that this Muster does not speak.
protocol_mismatch The answer does not match the protocol, or is larger than 64 KiB.
event_delivery_not_offered The bridge does not offer the Connection's event delivery mode.
proxy_refused The Connection's proxy refused its credentials (407).
timeout, connect_failed, dns_failed, tls_failed Muster got no answer from the bridge.
blocked The outbound address policy does not allow the bridge's address.
request_failed The bridge answered with another error; the message names its class and the bridge's text.

Add a Destination¶

A Bridge Destination is one channel of the messenger, named by its channel id — the channel as your messenger names it, which the bridge's documentation explains how to find. Its Mention settings take nobody or chosen users; everyone in the chat and messenger groups are not available through a bridge. Its limiter allows 30 requests per minute by default, sends and edits together.

curl -X POST https://muster.example.org/api/v1/destinations \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MUSTER_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"type":"bridge","name":"alerts","connection_id":"CNK7M3QX9P2RTA","channel_id":"C024BE91L",
       "mentions":{"new_alert_group":{"everyone":"none","user_ids":[],"groups":[]},
                   "new_alerts":{"everyone":"none","user_ids":[],"groups":[]},
                   "reopen":{"everyone":"none","user_ids":[],"groups":[]},
                   "ack_timeout":{"everyone":"none","user_ids":[],"groups":[]},
                   "snooze_ended":{"everyone":"none","user_ids":[],"groups":[]},
                   "rise_to_urgent":{"everyone":"none","user_ids":[],"groups":[]}},
       "limiter":{"limit":30,"per_seconds":60}}'

Saving the Destination runs its Destination check, the one item channel: the bridge's checkChannel, which posts nothing. It passes when the bot can post, edit and reply in the channel, and the Destination then shows the channel's name as the bridge reports it. It fails with the bridge's own text when the bridge refuses the channel — it does not exist, or the bot is not in it — with proxy_refused when the Connection's proxy refuses its credentials, and with the class of the bridge's answer otherwise; a Destination that fails the check is not saved. POST /api/v1/destinations/DS…/checks runs the same check at any time.

What Muster posts¶

  • The envelope. Every Root message carries its status with a colour and an emoji, the title #N and the Alert Group's title, the link "Open in Muster" to the Alert Group page, and its text in CommonMark: the labels, the summary, one line per Alert, the links, the notices and the footer. A Thread reply carries its text in CommonMark. Alert data are escaped, so a label value cannot format the message, link anywhere or mention anyone. A Loud message asks the bridge to notify; a Quiet one does not.
  • The length. The text fits the longest text the bridge declares, read from the Connection's copy of its info: a long Root message drops its Alert lines first, then the label sections, but always keeps its title, its status, its footer, its buttons and its link to Muster. The bridge may still shorten what it renders, and says so.
  • Buttons. When the bridge offers buttons, a Root message carries the buttons of its status — Ack, Resolve and one per Snooze duration, for example — with a style hint for each, at most as many as the bridge allows; the Snooze buttons of the longest durations go first. Without buttons, a bridge that forwards the messages of a Thread gets a last line in each Root message that is not resolved, such as "Reply in the Thread: ack · resolve · snooze 1h".
  • Edits and Thread replies. An edit sends the whole envelope of the new state, Quiet and without Mentions. A Thread reply goes under the Root message with the Thread reference the bridge returned last.
  • Posts are not repeated. Every new message carries an idempotency key, the same for every retry of it. When an answer is lost — a timeout, or a connection cut after the request — Muster retries with the same key, the bridge answers with the message it posted already, and Muster edits it to the current state. When the bridge did not know the key, the retry may have posted a second message: the Timeline records a possible duplicate.
  • The token never shows in a log line, an error, the Timeline or a metric; a test shows it as [redacted].

Test messages and previews¶

A test message checks how a Destination looks without touching any Alert Group:

curl -X POST https://muster.example.org/api/v1/destinations/DSK7M3QX9P2RTA/tests \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $MUSTER_TOKEN" -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"source":{"kind":"example"}}'

It posts one message of the kind test, Quiet, marked "🧪 Test message" before its text, with the buttons of its status, whose presses change nothing. It goes through the same limiters as delivery, and the result shows the step message with the request — the token masked — the bridge's answer and its error class. Use {"source":{"kind":"alert_group","alert_group_id":"AG…"}} to render a recent Alert Group of one of the Destination's Routes instead of the example. POST …/previews with the same body returns, without posting anything, the envelope the test would post, as JSON in the item message.

Broken Destinations¶

A Bridge Destination becomes Broken when the bridge refuses it — it answers forbidden, for example because the bot was removed from the channel — with the bridge's text as the reason, or when the bridge keeps failing or does not answer for the whole Transient budget: 10 attempts within 30 minutes. A bridge that asks Muster to wait does not make a Destination Broken unless it asks for more than 15 minutes; an answer the bridge calls invalid ends that one delivery as Not delivered and leaves the Destination healthy. When the bridge answers that a Root message is gone, Muster publishes the Alert Group again, once.

While a Destination is Broken, Muster tries again every 5 minutes: with the oldest waiting delivery, or, when nothing waits, with the Destination check, which posts nothing. The first success ends the Broken state, and so does a successful check or test message. See MusterDestinationBroken. A Bridge Connection whose bridge cannot be reached does not become Broken itself: its Destinations do, and its Connection check shows the cause.

Account links¶

A press or a Mention through a bridge names a person through their Account link for that Connection; see Link your messenger accounts.

Check from the command line¶

muster doctor prints one line per Bridge Connection, with the result of getInfo, and one line per Bridge Destination, with the result of checkChannel, without posting anything.
