Escalation chains¶

An Escalation chain Pages more and more people while an Alert Group stays firing. Its steps Page the On-call of a Schedule, chosen people or a whole Team, call through a Notification gateway, wait, send to an outgoing webhook and repeat. Routes and Teams name their chains. Chains are managed through the API under /api/v1/escalation-chains, listed in the API reference.

Chains run on Alert Groups at the points described under When chains run: their steps follow one another with their waits and repeats, and a step is skipped where it cannot be made. A webhook step sends its event to its outgoing webhook (Webhook steps). A step that Pages people or a Team writes a Mention in the Threads of its chain's own Destinations (Where chains Page); a step that calls through a Notification gateway, and the call actions of the Notification rules, call the person's confirmed contact (Calls through a Notification gateway). Where no chain runs, the ack timeout of a Route keeps working as before.

What an Escalation chain is¶

An Escalation chain has:

  • a name, unique in the Organization, compared without regard to case;
  • a description;
  • an optional owning Team, which organizes the chains — the list filters by Team, team=<public_id> or team=mine — and whose Working hours its steps use;
  • its steps, in their order, numbered from 1.

Only Admins create, change and delete chains (escalation-chains:write); everyone reads them (escalation-chains:read). A change is saved with If-Match, like every configuration, and is recorded in the Audit log as escalation_chain.created, escalation_chain.updated or escalation_chain.deleted with the values before and after. The metric muster_escalation_chain_info has one series per chain, with its name (Metrics).

A chain's read also lists the Routes and Teams that name it (referrers), the Destinations it writes in — those of those Routes and Teams (destinations) — and its warnings, what the chain or a step cannot do:

  • pages_nobody: no step of the chain Pages anyone — it has no step that Pages the On-call, chosen people or a Team, or calls, such as an empty chain or one of waits, repeats and webhooks. It concerns the whole chain, so its step is null, and it comes first. The chain is saved all the same;
  • everyone_unsupported: a step "Page the whole Team" whose Team mention, or word for everyone in the chat, the Connection of one of the chain's Destinations cannot mention, such as a Telegram Destination, with the Destination and the reason;
  • gateway_deleted: a call step whose Notification gateway was deleted, or is no Notification gateway any more;
  • destination_deleted: a webhook step whose Destination was deleted;
  • team_deleted: a step "Page the whole Team" whose Team was deleted since it was saved, instead of its everyone_unsupported warnings.

A chain that a Route or a Team names cannot be deleted: deleteEscalationChain answers 409 chain_in_use, naming them in in_use_by. A Team that owns a chain cannot be deleted either (409 team_in_use), and a Schedule that a step names cannot be deleted (409 schedule_in_use).

Steps¶

A chain has at most 20 steps (escalation.limits). Each step is of one kind and takes the fields of its kind only; a field of another kind is refused with 422 unsupported at it, and a missing one with required:

Kind What it does Its fields
page_on_call Pages the On-call of a Schedule of any Team schedule_id, importance
page_people Pages chosen people user_ids, importance
page_team Pages a whole Team by its Team mention, or everyone in the chat team_id or everyone
call Calls the On-call of a Schedule or chosen people through a Notification gateway call_target, schedule_id or user_ids, gateway_id
wait Waits a number of whole minutes wait_seconds
webhook Sends to an outgoing webhook Destination destination_id
repeat Goes back to an earlier step, a number of times repeat_from, repeat_times
  • Importance. A Page is normal or important, normal when omitted; it runs the person's Notification rule of that name.
  • People. A step names people who may be Paged: a Viewer or a disabled User that a save adds is refused with 422 not_pageable at /steps/{i}/user_ids/{j}, with the reason viewer or disabled. A person the stored steps already name is kept when the chain is saved again, even if they have become a Viewer or been disabled since; a running chain will skip them.
  • The whole Team. Without team_id and everyone, the step Pages the chain's owning Team; a chain without an owning Team must name the Team (required at team_id). everyone is channel, here, all or everyone.
  • Calls. call_target is on_call, with schedule_id, or people, with user_ids. A call step needs a Notification gateway: without one it is refused with 422 gateway_required; with one, gateway_id may be omitted and the step takes that gateway; with several, gateway_id is required.
  • Waits. A wait lasts from 1 to 1,440 minutes, given in seconds: wait_seconds is a multiple of 60 from 60 to 86400, so a wait of 10 minutes is {"kind": "wait", "wait_seconds": 600}. Another value is refused with 422 out_of_range at it.
  • Webhooks. The Destination of a webhook step is an outgoing webhook in the mode events or both; another one is refused with 422 webhook_events_required.
  • Repeats. repeat_from is the number of an earlier step, and repeat_times from 1 to 10.
  • Limits. Every step but wait and repeat takes working_hours_only — the step is made only in the Working hours of the chain's owning Team, else the Organization's — and a severity_condition, any by default, critical or below_critical.

A gateway or a webhook Destination that a stored step names may be deleted; the chain warns about it and can still be saved.

Webhook steps¶

A step "Send to an outgoing webhook" (webhook) sends the event escalation_webhook to the outgoing webhook Destination it names when the step is made. The event carries the Alert Group, as every event does, with the chain and the step. The Destination receives it whether or not the Alert Group is delivered there, so an outgoing webhook on none of the Alert Group's Routes can be called at a chosen step of a chain. Such a Destination receives no other event of the Alert Group, and the events-mode Destinations of the Alert Group's Routes do not receive escalation_webhook. A Destination that is also one of the Alert Group's receives it once, in order among its other events. The event is signed, ordered and delivered at least once like every event (escalation_webhook).

The Timeline records the Quiet escalation_webhook, of the kind escalation, with the chain, the step and the Destination; nothing appears in a messenger. Like every step that sends, a webhook step can be limited to Working hours and to a Severity condition. A running chain skips a webhook step, recording escalation_step_skipped, when its Destination:

  • was deleted since the step was saved: destination_deleted;
  • is no longer an outgoing webhook in the mode events or both, such as one switched to the template mode: webhook_events_required.

Naming chains on Routes and Teams¶

Routes and Teams name their chains in chain_entries, as they name their Destinations: each entry is a chain with a Severity condition — any, the default, critical or below_critical — checked against the Severity level of the Alert Group. An entry list replaces the stored one as a whole, and an update that omits it keeps it. Reads give the entries and the chains by name, chains, and the Audit log of the Route or the Team records the change.

{"chain_entries": [{"chain_id": "ECK7M3QX9P2RTA", "severity_condition": "critical"}]}

Naming a chain on a Route needs routes:write, and on a Team teams:write. A chain Pages only in the Threads of its own Destinations: a chain of a Route in those of the Route's Destinations that have the Alert Group's Root message; a chain of a Team in those of the Team's Destinations that have it, and, where none has, in those of the Route's Destinations. So a step that Pages everyone in the chat never rings another team's chat.

When chains run¶

The chains that run on an Alert Group are those its Route names and — where the Route has "Also to the Teams from the label" (deliver_to_teams) and "Run the Teams' Escalation chains" (run_team_chains) on — those that each of its Teams names, each whose Severity condition its Severity level meets. Several chains may run on one Alert Group at the same time. A chain that the Route and a Team both name, or several Teams, runs once, with all of them as its sources. With "Run the Teams' Escalation chains" off, the Teams' Destinations still receive the Alert Group, but their chains do not start.

A chain runs only while the Alert Group is firing, and only Alerts and Commands start chains:

Point What happens to the chains
The Alert Group's first Publication; for one that a Storm summary stood for, its own Publication; for an outgoing webhook in events mode, its delivered created event The chains that apply start at their first step
The Alert Group is created while no Destination reaches it, or only Broken ones The chains that apply start at once
It becomes firing again: a Reopen into firing, Unacknowledge, Unsnooze, the end of a Snooze, Unresolve, a rise to critical that ends an acknowledgement or a Snooze, an auto-unacknowledge, its Owner disabled or deleted The chains that apply start over at their first step
Its Severity level rises by its Alerts while it is firing and nobody owns it The chains whose condition it now meets start; running chains go on
A Team joins it by a new Alert while it is firing and nobody owns it That Team's chains start, where the Route runs the chains of Teams
A change of configuration: saving a Team, turning on "Also to the Teams from the label" or "Run the Teams' Escalation chains", naming a chain on a Route or a Team or changing its condition, changing the Minimum severity No chain starts, and the running chains go on
Acknowledge, Snooze, Resolve Every chain stops

A chain whose condition the Alert Group no longer meets after its Severity level falls goes on. A chain that a change of configuration would apply to starts at the next point that starts or starts over the chains, such as an Unacknowledge. A held Alert Group that a raised Minimum severity releases is published Quietly and starts its ack timeout, but no chain: that release is a change of configuration.

Each run keeps the steps its chain had when it started, until it stops or starts over: a change of the chain applies to the runs that start after it. A step that Pages or sends, limited to Working hours, is skipped outside the Working hours of the chain's owning Team, else the Organization's; a step whose Severity condition the Alert Group does not meet, a step that Pages the On-call of a Schedule with a gap now, and each person a step names who has become a Viewer or been disabled since the step was saved are skipped too. Each skip records the Quiet lifecycle event escalation_step_skipped in the Timeline, of the kind escalation, with the chain, the step and the reason — outside_working_hours, severity, schedule_gap, viewer or disabled, for a webhook step destination_deleted or webhook_events_required (Webhook steps), for a step "Page the whole Team" that none of its chain's Destinations can mention no_mention_possible (Where chains Page), and for a call step whose gateway was deleted gateway_deleted, or for a person it would call without a confirmed contact there no_confirmed_contact (Calls through a Notification gateway) — and the chain goes on with its next step at once. A wait delays the next step by its length on the clock of Muster, and "Repeat from step 3, up to 2 times" makes steps 3 to the repeat three times in all, then goes on. Outgoing webhooks in the mode events receive escalation_step_skipped with the chain, the step and the reason (Events).

Steps are timer rows that any replica claims, and each step is a change of its Alert Group made by Muster, so a replica that stops never stops a chain. getAlertGroup gives the runs of the chains since they last started in escalation_chains: each with its chain, its sources, its state — running, ran_out once it passed its last step, stopped by Acknowledge, Snooze or Resolve —, the step it is at and next_step_at, when its next step is due. listEscalationChainRuns (GET /api/v1/escalation-chains/{escalation_chain_id}/runs) lists the recent runs of a chain, newest first: each with its Alert Group, the steps it made and skipped with their reasons, and what stopped it — the Command and the person who ran it — or that it ran out. An Alert Group whose chains started over has a run per start.

Chains and the ack timeout¶

When the first chain of an Alert Group starts, its running ack timeout stops and sends no more notices: the steps take their place. When the Alert Group becomes firing again, its ack timeout starts over only if none of its chains starts. An Alert Group on which no chain applies keeps the ack timeout of its Route, as in L1 (Statuses and commands).

Where chains Page¶

A chain Pages only in the Threads of its own Destinations, so that a step that Pages everyone in the chat never rings another team's chat:

  • a chain of the Route, in those of the Route's Destinations that have the Alert Group's Root message;
  • a chain of a Team, in those of the Team's Destinations that have it, and, where none of them has, in those of the Route's Destinations;
  • a chain that the Route and a Team both name, which runs once, in the Threads of both.

A Destination whose first Publication of the Alert Group is still on its way counts as one with the Root message: the chains start with the first Publication in one Destination, and the Page waits there for the Root message to reply under. One that a Storm holds does not.

Which Destinations a Route or a Team reaches is the rule of Routing to Teams: the Severity condition of each entry against the Alert Group's Severity level. A Destination is the Route's or a Team's when that Route's or Team's own entry reached it, at a level the Alert Group had, and still names it. Destinations only grow, so a Destination stays the Route's or the Team's after the level falls: when the critical Alerts resolve and the level falls to warning, the chain of a Team still Pages in the Team's critical channel, which has the Root message, and does not fall back to the Route's. A channel that several name belongs only to those whose entry reached it: when Team devops names a shared channel for any level and Team payments names it for critical only, a warning Alert Group that was never critical has its Root message there by devops' entry, so the chain of payments does not Page there, and its @here never rings devops' channel. A Page is a Thread reply that delivery sends like any other: Loud, due at once without the Thread batching window, never combined with another reply. It says which step and which chain it comes from:

Step 1: @ivan, you are needed
Chain of the Route Infrastructure · On-call of devops Main · normal
  • The people. A step "Page the On-call of a Schedule" Pages the On-call of that moment, and a step "Page people" each person it names; each person by their Notification rule of the step's importance. The first Mentions of the rules that come due together are one reply per Destination naming them all. An important Page says "important, again in 5 min" when the person's rule Mentions them again — for a Page naming several people, only when every one of their rules does after the same time — and that later Mention repeats the first line with "· important, again". The rule stops when the Alert Group is acknowledged, snoozed or resolved, or when its chain starts over, and a Mention that comes due after that is not made.
  • A whole Team or everyone in the chat. A step "Page the whole Team" writes the Team's Team mention for the Destination's Connection — the chain's owning Team when the step names none, the owning Team the chain had when its run started — or the word for everyone in the chat, @here for example. Where a Destination's Connection can mention neither, the step makes no reply there; where it can reply in none of the chain's Destinations, as when the only one is a Telegram channel, it records escalation_step_skipped with no_mention_possible.
  • The Timeline. Each Page records the Loud lifecycle event paged, of the kind escalation, with its people, its chain and its step; outgoing webhooks in events mode receive it as every event, with its people as data (Events).

A Page by Mention replies in Mattermost, Slack and Telegram Destinations and through Bridges; in Telegram the reply is a comment under the channel post in the discussion group, as every Thread reply. An outgoing webhook in the template mode takes the Page through its "reply in thread" request, and one without that request takes none. One in the events mode has no Root message and takes no reply: it receives paged as an event, and only when the Page replies in another Destination of the chain. A chain whose only Destinations are outgoing webhooks in the events mode records no paged: a Page of people records page_not_delivered with no_root_message, and a step "Page the whole Team" escalation_step_skipped with no_mention_possible. A step "Page the whole Team" never replies in Telegram, which mentions no group and no one for everyone in the chat, and through a Bridge only when its bridge declares group mentions, for a Team mention, or the word for everyone in its everyone_mentions.

How a Page reaches a person¶

A Page names each person as a Mention only where they have an Account link in the Identity space of that Destination's Connection (Account links); a person without one is named in the line "Page not delivered: @petr has no linked Mattermost account" instead, which mentions nobody.

Where A Page is It reaches the person when
Mattermost a Thread reply with @username they have an Account link for the Connection
Slack a Thread reply with <@U…> they have an Account link for the Connection
Telegram a comment in the discussion group with a tg://user?id= link they have a Telegram Account link and are in the discussion group
Bridge a Thread reply with a Mention marker they have an Account link for the Connection and the bridge declares native_mentions
Outgoing webhook, events mode the event paged, with the people as mentions data, when the Page replies in another Destination of the chain the receiver acts on it
Outgoing webhook, template mode the "reply in thread" request, when it has one the receiver acts on it
Notification gateway a call to the confirmed contact the gateway reaches them, whatever the messengers do

In Telegram the reply reads "Step 1: Ivan, you are needed" with Ivan as a link that mentions him, and in a bridge "Step 1: @Ivan, you are needed" with his entry in the envelope's mentions (Rendering the envelope). A Mention in Telegram rings only a member of the discussion group: the creator of the group, anonymous in it, who muted it is not rung. A bridge without native_mentions shows a linked person's marker as their plain name, which rings nobody, so the Page is not delivered to them through that Destination: when no other Destination of the chain reaches them, it records page_not_delivered with not_delivered. The same holds for a person whose Mention a bridge's reply leaves out because its line of Mentions keeps only the whole Mentions that fit half of the bridge's body limit (Undelivered Mentions): the reply does not reach them and they do not become an Assignee. An outgoing webhook takes every person of the Page as data, linked or not, and the Team of a step "Page the whole Team" by its slug and name; the template mode gives them to the "reply in thread" request as .Mentions, with .Event paged (Template mode). One whose "reply in thread" request was removed after the Page was queued sends nothing, and that reply ends Not delivered.

At night, only a Notification gateway is reliable

Whether a Mention in a Thread pushes a notification to a phone is still to be verified for Mattermost and Slack. Until it is, rely on a call through a Notification gateway for Pages at night.

Calls through a Notification gateway¶

A step "Call through a Notification gateway" (call) calls the On-call of its Schedule at that moment, or each person it names who may still be Paged, through its gateway; a person whose Notification rule has a call action is called the same way when a step Pages them normally or importantly. Each call is a Page of its step, of one person: it records the Quiet lifecycle event paged in the Timeline, of the kind escalation, naming the person and the gateway in gateway, and writes nothing in any Thread — the Thread reply of the same step, when there is one, already names the person. The call goes to the person's confirmed contact at the gateway (Notification gateways), through the queue of calls: the first call to a person through a gateway goes at once, and the Pages that come due while a call to them waits, is being retried or within gateway.combine_window after the previous one ended go together in the next call, one call about all of their Alert Groups.

Calls depend on no Root message and on no messenger: they keep working while a messenger is down or a Destination is Broken, and a Route with a chain and no Destination starts its chain when the Alert Group is created, so that its call steps still call. Right before its first attempt, a call drops the Alert Groups on which the chain or the rule that Paged has stopped — acknowledged, snoozed, resolved or started over —, and a call left without any is not sent; a chain that started over and Paged again keeps its Alert Group in the call. Once every Alert Group of a call being retried has stopped, its retries end.

A call that the gateway accepted reaches the person: they become an Assignee of the chain, as by a delivered Mention (Assignees). A call that ends Not delivered — refused, an answer Muster cannot classify, the retry budget used up, a gateway Broken or deleted, or a contact removed before it went — records page_not_delivered with the chain, the step, the person, the gateway and the reason, and leaves the Assignees as they were; the chain and the rules go on either way. A call step whose gateway was deleted, or is no Notification gateway any more, is skipped with gateway_deleted; a person it would call who has no confirmed contact there is skipped with no_confirmed_contact, and the step with them when none is left. Calls count in muster_pages_total with the kind call, and "How it went" lists them as Pages of the kind call with their gateway.

Undelivered Pages¶

A Page that reaches its person nowhere records the Quiet page_not_delivered with the person and the reason:

  • no_root_message: none of the chain's Destinations has the Alert Group's Root message — such as a Route with a chain and no Destination, whose chains start when the Alert Group is created;
  • no_account_link: the person has no Account link in the Identity space of any of the chain's Destinations;
  • not_delivered: every Thread reply that would mention them ended Not delivered, was withheld while its Destination was Broken, was sent through a bridge with their Mention left out for length, or was never sent because its Root message — a first Publication, or one published again after it was deleted — ended Not delivered; a reply withheld while Broken is not sent after the Destination recovers. A reply queued before its Destination became Broken, such as one whose own attempt made it Broken, is withheld too as soon as the Destination is Broken: the Page does not wait for the recovery, and still reaches the people that its other, delivered replies mentioned.

A call through a Notification gateway that ends Not delivered records it too, with the reason of the call (Calls through a Notification gateway).

A Page counts in muster_pages_total{chain,kind,outcome} once it ended: delivered once a delivered reply mentioned one of its people — for a Team or everyone in the chat, once one of its replies was delivered — or, for a call, once the gateway accepted it, and not_delivered otherwise; a Page whose chain was deleted before it ended, and a call not made because its chain or rule stopped first, are not counted (Metrics).

Assignees¶

Until someone acknowledges, an Alert Group shows its Assignees: for each chain started on it, running or run out, the people its latest step Paging people by name — the On-call of a Schedule, chosen people or a call through a Notification gateway — reached, each with the owning Team of that chain. A person is reached once a delivered Thread reply mentions them, or a call to them that the gateway accepted, as the table of How a Page reaches a person says for each messenger — a bridge's reply that names them by the plain name reaches nobody; a person a Page did not reach (see Undelivered Pages) never becomes an Assignee.

How they change, for each chain on its own:

  • a later step that reaches someone replaces the chain's Assignees, a repeated step included;
  • another reply of the same step, and the later Mentions of that step's rules, add to them; the later Mentions of an earlier step's rules, and a reply of an earlier step that comes late, change nothing;
  • a step that reaches none of its people leaves them as they were, and a step that Pages a whole Team or everyone in the chat changes nothing;
  • a chain that runs out keeps its Assignees, so an Unclaimed Alert Group still shows whom it Paged;
  • Acknowledge, Snooze and Resolve clear them all.

Each change records the Quiet assignees_changed in the Timeline, of the kind escalation, with the Assignees after it, and edits the Root messages without notifying anyone. Nobody sets Assignees by hand, and being one gives no right of its own: any Responder may acknowledge, and whoever does becomes the Owner.

Before an acknowledgement, item 7 of the Root message names them — "Assignees: @ivan (devops), @petr (payments)" — each as a Mention where their account is linked in the Destination's Identity space, by name otherwise. After an acknowledgement that stopped running chains it reads "Acknowledged by @ivan · Escalation chains stopped": the Owner is named without a Team. The layout that a bridge receives names the Assignees in assignees with the Team in team, and with the ref of their Mention entry in a new message to a bridge with native_mentions.

The API reads them in assignees of an Alert Group (user, team, chain, since), and GET /api/v1/alert-groups?assignee=me lists the open Alert Groups where the calling User is an Assignee, by a User's public_id for anyone else, and GET /api/v1/alert-group-counts counts them with the same parameter; a Service account token gets 422 for me. Outgoing webhook events carry them in the assignees of alert_group.

Unclaimed¶

A chain has run out when it has passed its last step. An Alert Group with chains is Unclaimed once every chain started on it has run out without an acknowledgement: it records unclaimed, and the Root message and the UI show "Nobody has taken this", as after the last ack timeout notice. A chain that starts later on an Unclaimed Alert Group — for a Team that joins, or a rise — makes it no longer Unclaimed while that chain runs, and it is Unclaimed again, recording unclaimed again, once that chain has run out too. Acknowledge, Snooze and Resolve end Unclaimed.
