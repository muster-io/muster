Escalation chains¶

An Escalation chain Pages more and more people while an Alert Group stays firing. Its steps Page the On-call of a Schedule, chosen people or a whole Team, call through a Notification gateway, wait, send to an outgoing webhook and repeat. Routes and Teams name their chains. Chains are managed through the API under /api/v1/escalation-chains, listed in the API reference.

Chains run on Alert Groups at the points described under When chains run: their steps follow one another with their waits and repeats, and a step is skipped where it cannot be made. A webhook step sends its event to its outgoing webhook (Webhook steps). In this version a step that Pages is made without Paging anyone yet; the Pages by Mention and the calls follow in later versions. Where no chain runs, the ack timeout of a Route keeps working as before.

What an Escalation chain is¶

An Escalation chain has:

  • a name, unique in the Organization, compared without regard to case;
  • a description;
  • an optional owning Team, which organizes the chains — the list filters by Team, team=<public_id> or team=mine — and whose Working hours its steps use;
  • its steps, in their order, numbered from 1.

Only Admins create, change and delete chains (escalation-chains:write); everyone reads them (escalation-chains:read). A change is saved with If-Match, like every configuration, and is recorded in the Audit log as escalation_chain.created, escalation_chain.updated or escalation_chain.deleted with the values before and after. The metric muster_escalation_chain_info has one series per chain, with its name (Metrics).

A chain's read also lists the Routes and Teams that name it (referrers), the Destinations it writes in — those of those Routes and Teams (destinations) — and its warnings, what the chain or a step cannot do:

  • pages_nobody: no step of the chain Pages anyone — it has no step that Pages the On-call, chosen people or a Team, or calls, such as an empty chain or one of waits, repeats and webhooks. It concerns the whole chain, so its step is null, and it comes first. The chain is saved all the same;
  • everyone_unsupported: a step "Page the whole Team" whose Team mention, or word for everyone in the chat, the Connection of one of the chain's Destinations cannot mention, such as a Telegram Destination, with the Destination and the reason;
  • gateway_deleted: a call step whose Notification gateway was deleted, or is no Notification gateway any more;
  • destination_deleted: a webhook step whose Destination was deleted;
  • team_deleted: a step "Page the whole Team" whose Team was deleted since it was saved, instead of its everyone_unsupported warnings.

A chain that a Route or a Team names cannot be deleted: deleteEscalationChain answers 409 chain_in_use, naming them in in_use_by. A Team that owns a chain cannot be deleted either (409 team_in_use), and a Schedule that a step names cannot be deleted (409 schedule_in_use).

Steps¶

A chain has at most 20 steps (escalation.limits). Each step is of one kind and takes the fields of its kind only; a field of another kind is refused with 422 unsupported at it, and a missing one with required:

Kind What it does Its fields
page_on_call Pages the On-call of a Schedule of any Team schedule_id, importance
page_people Pages chosen people user_ids, importance
page_team Pages a whole Team by its Team mention, or everyone in the chat team_id or everyone
call Calls the On-call of a Schedule or chosen people through a Notification gateway call_target, schedule_id or user_ids, gateway_id
wait Waits a number of whole minutes wait_seconds
webhook Sends to an outgoing webhook Destination destination_id
repeat Goes back to an earlier step, a number of times repeat_from, repeat_times
  • Importance. A Page is normal or important, normal when omitted; it runs the person's Notification rule of that name.
  • People. A step names people who may be Paged: a Viewer or a disabled User that a save adds is refused with 422 not_pageable at /steps/{i}/user_ids/{j}, with the reason viewer or disabled. A person the stored steps already name is kept when the chain is saved again, even if they have become a Viewer or been disabled since; a running chain will skip them.
  • The whole Team. Without team_id and everyone, the step Pages the chain's owning Team; a chain without an owning Team must name the Team (required at team_id). everyone is channel, here, all or everyone.
  • Calls. call_target is on_call, with schedule_id, or people, with user_ids. A call step needs a Notification gateway: without one it is refused with 422 gateway_required; with one, gateway_id may be omitted and the step takes that gateway; with several, gateway_id is required.
  • Waits. A wait lasts from 1 to 1,440 minutes, given in seconds: wait_seconds is a multiple of 60 from 60 to 86400, so a wait of 10 minutes is {"kind": "wait", "wait_seconds": 600}. Another value is refused with 422 out_of_range at it.
  • Webhooks. The Destination of a webhook step is an outgoing webhook in the mode events or both; another one is refused with 422 webhook_events_required.
  • Repeats. repeat_from is the number of an earlier step, and repeat_times from 1 to 10.
  • Limits. Every step but wait and repeat takes working_hours_only — the step is made only in the Working hours of the chain's owning Team, else the Organization's — and a severity_condition, any by default, critical or below_critical.

A gateway or a webhook Destination that a stored step names may be deleted; the chain warns about it and can still be saved.

Webhook steps¶

A step "Send to an outgoing webhook" (webhook) sends the event escalation_webhook to the outgoing webhook Destination it names when the step is made. The event carries the Alert Group, as every event does, with the chain and the step. The Destination receives it whether or not the Alert Group is delivered there, so an outgoing webhook on none of the Alert Group's Routes can be called at a chosen step of a chain. Such a Destination receives no other event of the Alert Group, and the events-mode Destinations of the Alert Group's Routes do not receive escalation_webhook. A Destination that is also one of the Alert Group's receives it once, in order among its other events. The event is signed, ordered and delivered at least once like every event (escalation_webhook).

The Timeline records the Quiet escalation_webhook, of the kind escalation, with the chain, the step and the Destination; nothing appears in a messenger. Like every step that sends, a webhook step can be limited to Working hours and to a Severity condition. A running chain skips a webhook step, recording escalation_step_skipped, when its Destination:

  • was deleted since the step was saved: destination_deleted;
  • is no longer an outgoing webhook in the mode events or both, such as one switched to the template mode: webhook_events_required.

Naming chains on Routes and Teams¶

Routes and Teams name their chains in chain_entries, as they name their Destinations: each entry is a chain with a Severity condition — any, the default, critical or below_critical — checked against the Severity level of the Alert Group. An entry list replaces the stored one as a whole, and an update that omits it keeps it. Reads give the entries and the chains by name, chains, and the Audit log of the Route or the Team records the change.

{"chain_entries": [{"chain_id": "ECK7M3QX9P2RTA", "severity_condition": "critical"}]}

Naming a chain on a Route needs routes:write, and on a Team teams:write. A chain Pages only in the Threads of its own Destinations: a chain of a Route in those of the Route's Destinations that have the Alert Group's Root message; a chain of a Team in those of the Team's Destinations that have it, and, where none has, in those of the Route's Destinations. So a step that Pages everyone in the chat never rings another team's chat.

When chains run¶

The chains that run on an Alert Group are those its Route names and — where the Route has "Also to the Teams from the label" (deliver_to_teams) and "Run the Teams' Escalation chains" (run_team_chains) on — those that each of its Teams names, each whose Severity condition its Severity level meets. Several chains may run on one Alert Group at the same time. A chain that the Route and a Team both name, or several Teams, runs once, with all of them as its sources. With "Run the Teams' Escalation chains" off, the Teams' Destinations still receive the Alert Group, but their chains do not start.

A chain runs only while the Alert Group is firing, and only Alerts and Commands start chains:

Point What happens to the chains
The Alert Group's first Publication; for one that a Storm summary stood for, its own Publication; for an outgoing webhook in events mode, its delivered created event The chains that apply start at their first step
The Alert Group is created while no Destination reaches it, or only Broken ones The chains that apply start at once
It becomes firing again: a Reopen into firing, Unacknowledge, Unsnooze, the end of a Snooze, Unresolve, a rise to critical that ends an acknowledgement or a Snooze, an auto-unacknowledge, its Owner disabled or deleted The chains that apply start over at their first step
Its Severity level rises by its Alerts while it is firing and nobody owns it The chains whose condition it now meets start; running chains go on
A Team joins it by a new Alert while it is firing and nobody owns it That Team's chains start, where the Route runs the chains of Teams
A change of configuration: saving a Team, turning on "Also to the Teams from the label" or "Run the Teams' Escalation chains", naming a chain on a Route or a Team or changing its condition, changing the Minimum severity No chain starts, and the running chains go on
Acknowledge, Snooze, Resolve Every chain stops

A chain whose condition the Alert Group no longer meets after its Severity level falls goes on. A chain that a change of configuration would apply to starts at the next point that starts or starts over the chains, such as an Unacknowledge. A held Alert Group that a raised Minimum severity releases is published Quietly and starts its ack timeout, but no chain: that release is a change of configuration.

Each run keeps the steps its chain had when it started, until it stops or starts over: a change of the chain applies to the runs that start after it. A step that Pages or sends, limited to Working hours, is skipped outside the Working hours of the chain's owning Team, else the Organization's; a step whose Severity condition the Alert Group does not meet, a step that Pages the On-call of a Schedule with a gap now, and each person a step names who has become a Viewer or been disabled since the step was saved are skipped too. Each skip records the Quiet lifecycle event escalation_step_skipped in the Timeline, of the kind escalation, with the chain, the step and the reason — outside_working_hours, severity, schedule_gap, viewer or disabled, and for a webhook step destination_deleted or webhook_events_required (Webhook steps) — and the chain goes on with its next step at once. A wait delays the next step by its length on the clock of Muster, and "Repeat from step 3, up to 2 times" makes steps 3 to the repeat three times in all, then goes on. Outgoing webhooks in the mode events receive escalation_step_skipped with the chain, the step and the reason (Events).

Steps are timer rows that any replica claims, and each step is a change of its Alert Group made by Muster, so a replica that stops never stops a chain. getAlertGroup gives the runs of the chains since they last started in escalation_chains: each with its chain, its sources, its state — running, ran_out once it passed its last step, stopped by Acknowledge, Snooze or Resolve —, the step it is at and next_step_at, when its next step is due. listEscalationChainRuns (GET /api/v1/escalation-chains/{escalation_chain_id}/runs) lists the recent runs of a chain, newest first: each with its Alert Group, the steps it made and skipped with their reasons, and what stopped it — the Command and the person who ran it — or that it ran out. An Alert Group whose chains started over has a run per start.

Chains and the ack timeout¶

When the first chain of an Alert Group starts, its running ack timeout stops and sends no more notices: the steps take their place. When the Alert Group becomes firing again, its ack timeout starts over only if none of its chains starts. An Alert Group on which no chain applies keeps the ack timeout of its Route, as in L1 (Statuses and commands).

Until the Pages follow, a chain silences the ack timeout

In this version a step that Pages makes no Page yet, but the first chain that starts still removes the ack timeout. An Alert Group on a Route or a Team with a chain then gets neither ack timeout notices nor Pages. Until the Pages by Mention follow, name no Escalation chain on the Routes and Teams of an installation that relies on the ack timeout.

Unclaimed¶

A chain has run out when it has passed its last step. An Alert Group with chains is Unclaimed once every chain started on it has run out without an acknowledgement: it records unclaimed, and the Root message and the UI show "Nobody has taken this", as after the last ack timeout notice. A chain that starts later on an Unclaimed Alert Group — for a Team that joins, or a rise — makes it no longer Unclaimed while that chain runs, and it is Unclaimed again, recording unclaimed again, once that chain has run out too. Acknowledge, Snooze and Resolve end Unclaimed.
