Runbooks¶

One page for every alert Muster can raise: the rules of the Helm chart, evaluated by Prometheus or VictoriaMetrics from Muster's metrics, and the Internal alerts that Muster raises itself. Each page says what the alert means, its impact, how to diagnose it and how to fix it.

Alert Severity Source
MusterDown critical chart rule
MusterNoLeader critical chart rule
MusterDeliveryFailing critical chart rule
MusterDestinationBroken critical both
MusterHeartbeatLost critical both
MusterDeliverySlow warning chart rule
MusterDeliveryQueueGrowing warning chart rule
MusterIngestBacklog warning chart rule
MusterIngestRejected warning chart rule
MusterTemplateError warning both
MusterClockSkew warning chart rule
MusterLoginFailures warning chart rule
MusterSnapshotTruncated warning Internal alert
MusterOIDCSecretExpiring warning Internal alert

Two sources¶

  • Chart rules are evaluated by your monitoring from the metrics Muster serves at /metrics, and reach people through your Alertmanager like any other rule. Their thresholds, windows and job selector are values of the chart; the expressions on these pages are the defaults.
  • Internal alerts are raised by Muster itself, as Alerts of the built-in Integration "Muster" that every Organization has. Ordinary Routes pick them by their labels and deliver them like any other Alert Group; see Internal alerts.
  • An alert listed as both is an Internal alert with a chart rule of the same name for the same condition. The chart rule is the backup for when Muster cannot deliver the Internal alert itself.

Send the critical chart rules through an Alertmanager route that bypasses Muster, to a pager or a chat that Muster does not manage: when Muster is down or cannot deliver, it cannot deliver an alert about that either.

Runbook links¶

Every Internal alert carries a runbook_url annotation that opens its page here, for the version of Muster that raised it, and the chart rules link to the same pages:

<MUSTER_RUNBOOK_BASE_URL>/<version>/operations/runbooks/<alertname>/

MUSTER_RUNBOOK_BASE_URL is https://muster-io.github.io/muster by default, the published site; point it at your own copy of the site where people cannot reach the public one. <version> is the major.minor of the binary, such as 1.2, or latest for a development build. For example, MusterOIDCSecretExpiring of a development build links to https://muster-io.github.io/muster/latest/operations/runbooks/MusterOIDCSecretExpiring/.
