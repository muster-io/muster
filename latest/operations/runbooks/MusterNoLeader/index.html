MusterNoLeader¶

Meaning¶

Every replica of Muster reports that it is not the Leader. The chart rule is:

max(muster_leader{job="muster"}) == 0

It fires after 2 minutes, with severity critical. It is a chart rule only. One replica, the Leader, holds a lock in the database and runs the Leader tasks; when it cannot confirm its lock within leader.fencing_timeout (15 s) it stops them, and any replica may take the lock. This alert means that none has for 2 minutes.

Route it around Muster

Send this alert through an Alertmanager route that bypasses Muster. Muster still delivers without a Leader, but the problem behind the missing Leader — usually the database — may stop delivery next.

Impact¶

What stops while no replica leads:

  • Telegram polling: Connections in the long polling update mode receive no button presses and no messages from the discussion group.
  • Bridge polling: Bridge Connections in the poll event delivery mode receive no events — no button presses, Text commands, Account link requests or reports of deleted messages — until a replica leads again. A press or a Text command that is older than bridge.event_max_age (1 hour) by then is dropped unanswered.
  • The Heartbeat check and the Stale check: no Integration becomes Heartbeat lost, and no alert is resolved as Stale.
  • Partition maintenance, retention and the pruning of short-lived state, the outgoing heartbeat, the expiry check of the OIDC client secret and the Leader-only metrics (muster_alert_groups, muster_delivery_queue, muster_destination_broken, muster_heartbeat_lost, muster_ingest_backlog, muster_storm_active), which go stale or disappear.

What keeps working: ingestion, delivery to every Destination, timers (the end of a Snooze, Ack timeouts and Reminders), the UI, the API, the webhook update mode of Telegram and the push event delivery mode of Bridge Connections. After leader.absence_notice (2 minutes) without a Leader, the UI shows the banner "No replica is leading: Telegram and Bridge polling, Heartbeat and Stale checks are paused."

Diagnosis¶

  1. Open System status (/admin/system-status). Replicas lists every replica that refreshed its record, when it last did ("Refreshed …"), and which one is the Leader; with no Leader, no replica carries the flag.
  2. Check muster_leader per replica: max by (instance) (muster_leader{job="muster"}). A replica that does not appear at all is not scraped; see MusterDown.
  3. Read the logs of the replicas:
    • leadership_lost (replica, error): the Leader could not confirm its lock within leader.fencing_timeout, or its lock session failed.
    • leadership_acquired (replica): a replica took the lock. Alternating lost and acquired lines mean that the lock keeps breaking.
    • leader_task_failed (task, error): a Leader task failed, usually because the database was unavailable.
  4. Run muster doctor with the replicas' settings. session_connection must say "advisory locks and LISTEN work": the Leader lock is an advisory lock on the session connection. Behind a pooler in transaction mode, such as PgBouncer, advisory locks do not hold, and MUSTER_DATABASE_SESSION_URL must point at the database directly or at a pool in session mode.
  5. Check the database itself: its availability, failovers, connection limits and the latency between the replicas and the database.

Fix¶

  • Restore the database or the network path to it. Once the session connection works again, a replica takes the lock within seconds and the banner disappears.
  • Set MUSTER_DATABASE_SESSION_URL when the main connection goes through a pooler in transaction mode, and restart the replicas.
  • Raise the database's connection limit if the session connections are refused: each replica holds about three of them besides its main pool.
  • Once a Leader returns, the paused tasks run again at their next interval: the new Leader polls Telegram and the bridges, and the Heartbeat and Stale checks resume from the signals and Snapshots that ingestion recorded meanwhile.
