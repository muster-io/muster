MusterNotificationGatewayBroken¶

Meaning¶

A Notification gateway is Broken: a call through it ended with a Fatal error — the bridge, or the provider behind it, refused the token or the credentials (401 or 403, the class forbidden), or the outbound address policy blocks the bridge's address — or a call failed until its retry budget ran out (delivery.transient_budget, 10 attempts within 30 minutes). Muster then probes the gateway every delivery.broken_probe_interval (5 minutes) by reading its info, and ends the Broken state on the first success. A successful check of its Connection ends it too.

This alert has two sources with the same name, severity critical and this page:

  • The Internal alert, raised by Muster itself as an Alert of the built-in "Muster" Integration when the gateway becomes Broken, with the labels connection (the id of the gateway's Connection) and connection_name. It is resolved when a probe or a Connection check succeeds, and when the Connection is deleted. The suggested Route "Muster internal alerts" groups by connection, so every Broken gateway has an Alert Group of its own.

  • The chart rule, the backup for when Muster cannot deliver the Internal alert:

    max by (connection) (muster_notification_gateway_broken{job="muster"}) == 1

    It fires after 5 minutes with the label connection. muster_notification_gateway_broken is set by the Leader every 15 seconds, so the rule fires about 5 minutes after the gateway became Broken and resolves within 15 seconds after the state ended.

Route it around the gateway

A Broken gateway cannot call anybody about itself. Route the Internal alert to a messenger Destination, and send the chart rule through an Alertmanager route that bypasses Muster.

Impact¶

  • No call goes through the gateway. Every call that comes due while it is Broken — from an Escalation step or a Notification rule — ends Not delivered: each of its Alert Groups records page_not_delivered with the reason gateway_broken in its Timeline, naming the person and the gateway.
  • The calls that came due meanwhile were not sent, and are not sent after the gateway recovers. Nobody was called for those Alert Groups through this gateway; check them by hand.
  • The Destinations of the same bridge, if it is also a messenger, are not affected: a Broken gateway never makes a Destination Broken, nor the other way round. Other gateways, ingestion, delivery to messengers and timers keep working.

Diagnosis¶

  1. Open the gateway's Connection page (/connections/<id>). Its read in the API, GET /api/v1/connections/<id>, has gateway with broken, broken_since, reason and next_probe_at. The reason is the error the bridge answered to the call or to the last probe, with secrets and the contacts and texts of calls masked; it starts with "unavailable after repeated failures:" when the retry budget ran out.
  2. Open the tab Request log of the Connection and narrow it to the operations sendNotification and getInfo (see Request log): the record shows the status and the body the bridge answered. The probes are the getInfo requests of the kind Broken probe.
  3. Find the log line gateway_broken (connection, cause, reason): cause is fatal after a Fatal answer and unavailable after a call used up its retry budget. The lines gateway_call_not_delivered with the reason gateway_broken name the calls that were not sent.
  4. Run muster bridge check <base URL> --token-file <file> --contact <your contact> --no-interactive, and --channel <id> when the bridge is also a messenger, against the bridge to see which step of the Bridge protocol fails, outside Muster: it sends one test notification to your contact (see muster bridge check).

Fix¶

  • cause fatal, a 401 or 403: the bridge refuses Muster's token, or the provider refuses the bridge's credentials. Replace the token on the Connection if it is Muster's that changed; otherwise fix the credentials or the account of the provider in the bridge's own configuration — an expired API key, a blocked account, a used-up balance.
  • cause fatal without a status, the reason naming the outbound address policy: Muster refused to reach the bridge's address. Allow it in the Outbound addresses (/admin/organization/outbound), or fix the base URL of the Connection.
  • cause unavailable: the bridge or its provider did not answer, or answered transient or asked to wait, until the retry budget ran out. Fix the bridge, its provider, the network path to it or the proxy of the Connection.
  • Then press Check connection on the Connection page: a check whose step info passes ends the Broken state at once. Otherwise the next probe ends it by itself, within delivery.broken_probe_interval.
  • A passing info does not prove that calls go through. The check and the probe read only the bridge's info, which a bridge answers also while its provider refuses the bridge's own credentials, so the state can end while calls would still fail and make the gateway Broken again. Before you trust a recovery, run muster bridge check <base URL> --token-file <file> --contact <your contact> against the bridge, with --channel <id> when it is also a messenger: its step notification sends a real notification to your contact through the provider (see muster bridge check).
  • The Internal alert resolves and gateway_recovered (connection, by — probe or check — and broken_for_s) is logged when the Broken state ends; the chart rule resolves at the Leader's next update of muster_notification_gateway_broken, within 15 seconds.
  • Calls that come due afterwards go as usual. Check the Alert Groups whose Timeline shows page_not_delivered with gateway_broken: their people were not called.
