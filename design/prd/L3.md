# Muster L3 — Incidents

- Status: Draft — scope only
- Date: 2026-10-02

L3 adds **Incidents**: a coordinated response to a problem that may span several Alert Groups. It aims at parity with
the incident management of Grafana IRM. Nothing in this layer is designed yet; this file records the boundaries that
earlier layers already rely on. Terms follow [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md).

## Boundaries already fixed

- An **Alert Group is never an Incident.** The word Incident is reserved for this layer; an Incident may include several
  Alert Groups, and an Alert Group exists without one.
- An Incident has its own lifecycle and record; it does not change the four statuses of the Alert Groups it includes.
- Paging people for an Incident builds on L2 schedules and escalations.
- Its record builds on L1 Timelines and Notes and on L2 mirroring of messenger Threads.
- Summaries and post-incident review drafts come from L4, once Incidents exist.

## Candidate scope

To be decided when the layer starts:

- declaring an Incident from one or more Alert Groups, in the UI, the API and from a messenger;
- roles in an Incident, such as the person coordinating it, and its own status and severity;
- one Timeline that combines the Alert Groups' Timelines, Notes and messenger discussion;
- status updates sent to Destinations;
- closing an Incident with a post-incident review.
